# Sentiment Aura 1-Minute Demo Script

**Total read time: ~60 seconds**

---

## Opening (Calm, Peaceful - 10s)

"I'm feeling incredibly peaceful right now. Everything feels so serene and tranquil, like I'm floating in a gentle, meditative state."

*Expected: Low particle count, slow movement, cool blues/teals, circular or breathing pattern*

---

## Rising Energy (Positive, Excited - 15s)

"But wait, now I'm getting really excited! This is absolutely exhilarating - I feel vibrant and electric, like pure euphoric energy is coursing through me. This is amazing!"

*Expected: Particle count increases, faster speed, bright warm colors (oranges/yellows), outward or burst pattern*

---

## Emotional Shift (Anxious, Chaotic - 15s)

"Hold on... something feels off. I'm getting anxious now, really tense and uncertain. Everything feels scattered and chaotic, like I'm losing control and spiraling into confusion."

*Expected: Erratic movement, higher turbulence, darker/cooler colors, low coherence, jittery or frantic pattern*

---

## Melancholic Transition (Sad, Heavy - 12s)

"Now it's just... heavy. I feel melancholic and somber, like a deep wistfulness is washing over me. Everything seems muted and subdued."

*Expected: Slower movement, dim particles, desaturated blues/purples, low brightness, low density*

---

## Recovery to Hope (Hopeful, Warm - 8s)

"But you know what? There's still hope. I can feel warmth returning, a gentle optimism rising. Maybe things will be okay after all."

*Expected: Gradual brightness increase, warmer colors returning, moderate coherence, breathing or gentle flow*

---

## Notes

- Pause 1-2 seconds between sections to let the visualization update
- Speak naturally and with emotion to match the content
- Watch the keywords panel to see emotional descriptors being extracted
- Observe color transitions, particle behavior changes, and flow pattern shifts
- Total runtime: 60 seconds + pauses between sections
